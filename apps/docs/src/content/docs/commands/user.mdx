`/user` is your settings panel. It has two subcommands: `/user settings`
opens the panel, and `/user delete` jumps straight to data deletion.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Preferences">
    The first page covers how Parley treats you in play.

    | Setting | Options | What it does |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Matchmaking | Enabled, Disabled | Join the ranked queue, or pause it. Disabled means Parley never puts you into a match. |
    | Visibility | Public, Hidden | Control who sees your profile and stats. |

    Pick an option from each menu and the change saves immediately.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Privacy and data">
    The second page covers your data.

    - **Refresh stats** pulls the latest data from the stats provider for
      your linked account.
    - **Delete my data** anonymizes your Parley records. See below for what
      that involves.

    The page also shows your re-link cooldown when one is active, so you
    always know when you could link again.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Delete your data

<Aside type="danger" title="Deletion is permanent">
  Deleting anonymizes every record Parley holds about you and starts a
  30-day re-link cooldown. You cannot undo it.
</Aside>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the confirmation">
    Use **Delete my data** on the privacy page, or run `/user delete`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your Discord username">
    The form asks you to type your exact Discord username. Anything else is
    rejected and nothing is deleted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the summary">
    Parley confirms with your anonymized id, the time your cooldown ends,
    and a count of every record anonymized: players, identities, memberships,
    matches, participants, and tournament rows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

More detail on what Parley stores lives on the
[privacy and data](/privacy-and-data) page.
