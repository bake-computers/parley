`/profile` shows every game account linked to your Discord user, one line per
profile.

## What you see

Each linked account appears as a row with:

| Field       | Example                                        |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Game        | Rainbow Six Siege                              |
| Username    | your in-game name                              |
| Platform    | PC, Xbox, or PlayStation                       |
| Parley Rank | your tier and points, for example Gold · 2,540 |

<Aside type="tip" title="No linked accounts?">
  If the card says **No Linked Accounts**, run [/link](/commands/link) to connect your first
  platform. It takes about a minute.
</Aside>

## About the rank shown

The Parley Rank is Parley's own rating. It starts from your Rainbow Six rank
when you link and moves as you play Parley matches. It does not change your
Ubisoft rank, and your Ubisoft rank does not change it. The full ladder is on
the [Parley Rank](/ranks-and-stats/parley-rank) page.

<Aside type="note" title="Looking for deeper stats?">
  Your level, current Rainbow Six rank, and RP appear on the card you get right after linking. See
  [your stats](/ranks-and-stats/your-stats) for what Parley tracks and how it is used.
</Aside>
