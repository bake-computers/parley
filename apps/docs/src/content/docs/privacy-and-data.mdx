Parley keeps the minimum it needs to rate you and run matches. This page
covers what that is and the controls you have.

## What Parley stores

| Data                              | Why                                                    |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Your Discord id                   | Connects you to your game accounts                     |
| Linked profiles, one per platform | Pulls stats and identifies you in matches              |
| Public game stats                 | Powers your skill rating and team balance              |
| Match records and results         | Drives rank movement and [/history](/commands/history) |
| Your settings                     | Matchmaking opt-out and profile visibility             |

<Aside type="note" title="Only public game data">
  Parley reads the same Rainbow Six profile anyone can look up. It never sees your Ubisoft password
  or email, and it does not read your Discord messages.
</Aside>

## What you control

- **Visibility**: set your profile to Hidden in [/user](/commands/user) and
  your profile and stats stop being shown to others.
- **Matchmaking**: opt out any time in [/user](/commands/user). Parley stops
  putting you into matches immediately.

## Delete everything

<Aside type="danger" title="This anonymizes your records">
  Deletion replaces your identity in every record Parley holds with an anonymous id and starts a
  30-day re-link cooldown. It cannot be undone.
</Aside>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start deletion">
    Use **Delete my data** on the privacy page of [/user](/commands/user), or run `/user delete`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your username">
    Type your exact Discord username in the form. A mismatch rejects the request and deletes
    nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    Parley shows your anonymized id, when the cooldown ends, and the count of anonymized records
    across players, identities, memberships, matches, participants, and tournaments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the cooldown ends you may link a new account and start fresh. The
waiting period exists so deletion cannot be used to dodge a rating.
