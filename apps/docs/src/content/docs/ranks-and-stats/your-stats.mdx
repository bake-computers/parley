When you link, and whenever you refresh, Parley pulls your public Rainbow Six
Siege profile from the stats provider. You see the highlights right away:
your profile level, your current Siege rank, and your rank points.

<Aside type="tip" title="Refresh from /user">
  Your stats do not need manual upkeep for matchmaking, but you can pull the latest snapshot any
  time with **Refresh stats** on the [/user](/commands/user) privacy page.
</Aside>

## What Parley tracks

Behind the profile card, Parley keeps the season stats that describe how you
actually play:

| Signal                  | In plain terms                                              |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| K/D and kills per round | How often you win gunfights                                 |
| Win rate                | How often your team closes out matches                      |
| Survival rate           | How often you stay alive into the late round                |
| Headshot percentage     | Shot discipline and precision                               |
| KOST                    | How often you contribute a kill, assist, survival, or trade |
| Rank points             | Where Siege itself rates you                                |

<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>How Parley turns this into skill</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    Parley blends the signals above into one performance score, weighted so
    that winning and killing efficiently matter most, with survival and
    precision filling in the picture. Recent form weighs more than older
    seasons, so a player on a hot streak is rated at their current level,
    not last year's.

    You never see this score as a number. Its only job is to tell
    [matchmaking](/matchmaking) how strong you are right now, so teams come
    out even. Your visible rating is always the
    [Parley Rank](/ranks-and-stats/parley-rank).

  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

<Aside type="note" title="Only public data">
  Parley reads the same profile anyone can look up. It never sees your password, your email, or
  anything outside your public game profile. The [privacy page](/privacy-and-data) covers what is
  stored.
</Aside>
