Every linked player has a Parley Rank: a tier plus a points value. It is the
single number matchmaking and tournament seeding trust.

## The ladder

| Tier | Starts at |
| --- | --- |
| Iron | 1,000 points |
| Bronze | 1,400 points |
| Silver | 1,900 points |
| Gold | 2,400 points |
| Platinum | 2,900 points |
| Diamond | 3,400 points |
| Master | 3,900 points |
| Grandmaster | 4,400 points |
| Challenger | 4,900 points |

New accounts that have not played a Parley match yet show as **Unspecified**
until their first rating lands.

## Where your rank starts

When you [link your account](/getting-started/link-your-account), Parley
seeds your points from your current Rainbow Six rank points. A Diamond Siege
player starts as a Diamond in Parley, so matched play feels right from day
one.

## How it moves

Your rank updates when a Parley match is reported:

- **Wins add points, losses remove them.** How much depends on the expected
  result. Beating a stronger team moves you more than beating a weaker one.
- **Performance counts too.** Playing well above your usual level in a match
  gives the update a small extra nudge.
- **Nothing changes between matches.** Queueing, linking, or refreshing
  stats does not touch your rank.

<Aside type="tip" title="Check yours any time">
  Run `/profile` or open the [/link](/commands/link) hub to see your current
  tier and points on every linked platform.
</Aside>
