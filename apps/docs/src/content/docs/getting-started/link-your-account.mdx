Linking connects your Discord identity to your Rainbow Six Siege profile so
Parley can pull your stats and rate your skill. The whole flow is interactive:
no command arguments, just menus.

## Walk through the wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run /link">
    Parley opens the **Link Your Accounts** hub, which lists every platform
    you have linked so far. Choose **Add a platform** and pick PC, Xbox, or
    PlayStation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your in-game name">
    A small form asks for your in-game username. Use the exact name from your
    platform, shown below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Parley shows a confirmation card with your platform, username, and game.
    Select **Continue** to link, or **Go back** to start over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your stats">
    Parley fetches your profile from the stats provider. After a moment you
    get a **You're Ready To Play** card with your Parley Rank, profile level,
    and current Rainbow Six rank and RP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which name to enter

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="PC">
    Your Ubisoft Connect username. If you play through Steam, use the Ubisoft
    account name, not your Steam name.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Xbox">
    Your Xbox gamertag. This covers Xbox consoles and PC Game Pass accounts.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="PlayStation">
    Your PlayStation Network online ID.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## See your links again

The `/link` hub is also where you manage existing links. It shows each linked
profile with its platform, username, and Parley Rank, and it has a menu for
removing a link.

<Aside type="caution" title="Re-link cooldown">
  After you remove a link, Parley makes you wait before linking again on that
  platform. The hub tells you exactly when re-linking becomes available. This
  keeps people from hopping between accounts to reset their rating.
</Aside>

## If linking fails

<Aside type="note" title="Link Failed">
  A red **Link Failed** card means the stats provider could not resolve your
  username. Check the spelling, confirm the platform matches where you
  actually play, and try again.
</Aside>
