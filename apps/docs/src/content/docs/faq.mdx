<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>Which games does Parley support?</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    Rainbow Six Siege. One game, done properly. You can link one profile per platform: PC, Xbox, and
    PlayStation.
  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>Who can see my command replies?</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    Only you. Every Parley response is an ephemeral message, so your linked accounts, settings, and
    data never show up in the channel for others.
  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>Is my Parley Rank the same as my Ubisoft rank?</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    No. Your [Parley Rank](/ranks-and-stats/parley-rank) starts from your Rainbow Six rank when you
    link, then moves only from Parley matches. Ranked play in Siege does not touch it, and it never
    touches your Siege rank.
  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>How do I stop being matched?</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    Open [/user](/commands/user) and set matchmaking to Disabled. Parley stops including you in team
    building right away, and you can re-enable it the same way.
  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>Why is there a cooldown after unlinking or deleting?</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    Without a waiting period, anyone could reset a bad rating by unlinking and re-linking. The
    cooldown keeps ratings honest. The exact end time is always shown on the card that starts it.
  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>Why does /history not work in DMs?</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    Match history belongs to a server, so the command only runs in server channels. Run it in the
    server whose matches you want to see.
  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
  <CollapsibleTrigger>How do I leave Parley for good?</CollapsibleTrigger>
  <CollapsibleContent>
    Run `/user delete` and confirm with your Discord username. Every record is anonymized and a
    30-day re-link cooldown starts. Details are on the [privacy and data](/privacy-and-data) page.
  </CollapsibleContent>
</Collapsible>

Still stuck? Ask in the Parley community, or reach the team through
[parley.bot](https://parley.bot).
