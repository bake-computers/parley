`/user` is your settings panel. Use `/user settings` for the full hub, or
`/user delete` to jump straight into data deletion. Private to you.

## Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open settings">
    Run `/user settings`. Page 1 is **Preferences**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Matchmaking and visibility">
    Pick from each menu — changes save immediately.

    <UserPreferencesDemo />

    | Setting     | Options           | Effect                                              |
    | ----------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
    | Matchmaking | Enabled, Disabled | Join the ranked queue, or pause it entirely         |
    | Visibility  | Public, Hidden    | Who can see your profile and stats                  |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Privacy">
    Press **Privacy** to go to page 2 (stats refresh and data deletion).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Privacy and data

<UserPrivacyDemo />

- **Refresh stats** — pull the latest snapshot from the stats provider for
  your linked account.
- **Delete my data** — anonymize every Parley record about you (see below).
- **Preferences** — jump back to page 1.

The card also shows any active re-link cooldown so you know when you could
link again.

## Delete your data

<Aside type="danger" title="Deletion is permanent">
  Deleting anonymizes every record Parley holds about you and starts a 30-day re-link cooldown. You
  cannot undo it.
</Aside>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start deletion">
    Press **Delete my data** on the privacy page, or run `/user delete`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your Discord username">
    Type your exact Discord username in the form. Anything else is rejected and
    nothing is deleted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    Parley confirms with an anonymized id, when the cooldown ends, and how many
    rows were touched (players, identities, matches, tournaments, and so on).

    <UserDeletedDemo />
  </Step>
</Steps>
