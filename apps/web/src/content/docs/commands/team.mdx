`/team` runs a **custom match** with a lobby you choose. Parley splits the
players into balanced teams, you log rounds as you play, and ending the match
updates ratings — same idea as a queued match.

<Aside type="caution" title="Servers only">
  `/team` does not work in DMs. Run it in a channel of the server the match belongs to.
</Aside>

## How a match flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start /team and pick players">
    Run `/team`, then select **2–10 players** (even number) from the user picker.

    <TeamSetupDemo />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Play with the scoreboard">
    Parley builds **Team A** and **Team B**. After each round, hit **Log round**
    for that team and enter each player's line as `kills deaths assists`
    (example: `4 5 2`). Leave a field empty for zeros.

    <TeamScoreboardDemo />

    Only people in the match can log rounds or end it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="End the match">
    When you are done, press **End match** and pick the winner.

    <TeamEndDemo />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ratings update">
    The final card shows totals. Everyone's rating moves from the outcome.
    The match also appears in `/history`.

    <TeamFinalDemo />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ratings vs K/D/A

Ending the match updates Glicko-2 from the win/loss and lobby balance — same
as queued matchmaking. The K/D/A you log is stored on the match for history
and bragging rights; it does **not** drive the rating math.

<Aside type="tip" title="Eligibility still applies">
  Custom matches respect your server's matchmaking rules and daily quotas. If someone you pick is not
  eligible, the card explains why.
</Aside>
