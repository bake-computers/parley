`/team` runs a custom match with your own lobby. Parley's OVI model splits
the players you pick into balanced teams, you log the rounds as you play,
and ending the match updates everyone's rating — just like a queued match.

<Aside type="caution" title="Servers only">
  `/team` does not work in direct messages. Run it in a channel of the server the match belongs to.
</Aside>

## How a match flows

1. Run `/team` and pick **2–10 players** (an even number) from the picker.
2. Parley assembles **Team A** and **Team B** and shows the scoreboard.
3. After each round, click **Log round** for one of the teams and enter
   each player's line as `kills deaths assists` — for example `4 5 2`.
   Leave a player's field empty to count zeros.
4. Repeat for as many rounds as you play. The scoreboard keeps running
   K/D/A totals for every player.
5. Click **End match**, pick the winning team, and the card flips to the
   final result.

The finished match shows up in `/history` alongside queued matches, with
its K/D/A totals attached.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can log and end">
  Only players who were picked into the match can log rounds or end it — spectators clicking the
  buttons get a polite no.
</Aside>

## How ratings work

Ending the match updates each player's Glicko-2 rating from the outcome and
the lobby's pre-match balance rating, exactly like queued matchmaking. The
K/D/A you log is recorded for the match record and bragging rights; it does
not change the rating math.

<Aside type="tip" title="Eligibility still applies">
  Custom matches respect your server's matchmaking rules and daily quotas. If a picked player is not
  eligible, the card tells you why.
</Aside>
