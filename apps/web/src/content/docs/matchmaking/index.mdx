Parley exists because stacking teams by hand is slow and rarely fair. When
your server runs Parley matches, the teams come pre-balanced from everyone's
real skill data, so lobbies are decided before the argument starts.

## What balanced means here

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Even win odds" icon="ph:scales">
    Parley compares full rosters, not averages, and picks the split where both sides have the
    closest shot at winning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Form over reputation" icon="ph:trend-up">
    Ratings lean on recent performance. A player climbing fast is matched at their current level,
    not an old one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ranks that move" icon="ph:medal">
    Every reported match updates your [Parley Rank](/docs/ranks-and-stats/parley-rank), so the balance
    keeps improving as your server plays.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What you see as a player

- **The match itself**, with teams that were built for even odds.
- **An MVP**, picked from the match when one player clearly carried.
- **The record** in [/history](/docs/commands/history), with winners bolded and
  relative timestamps.
- **Rating movement** on your [Parley Rank](/docs/ranks-and-stats/parley-rank)
  after the result lands.

<Aside type="note" title="Opt out any time">
  Matchmaking respects your settings. Turn matchmaking off in [/user](/docs/commands/user) and Parley
  leaves you out of team building until you turn it back on.
</Aside>

## When you cannot play

Servers can set limits on who joins their matches, and Parley enforces them
before teams are built. If you are left out, the
[eligibility page](/docs/matchmaking/eligibility) lists every reason and the fix.
