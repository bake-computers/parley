Parley keeps two layers of numbers: your **Parley Rank**, the tier you see
everywhere, and the **stats** underneath it, pulled from your Rainbow Six
Siege profile.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Parley Rank" icon="ph:medal">
    Ten tiers from Iron to Challenger. Starts from your Rainbow Six rank and moves as you play
    Parley matches. [See the ladder](/docs/ranks-and-stats/parley-rank).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your stats" icon="ph:chart-line-up">
    The profile data behind the rating, and how Parley uses it to balance teams. [See what is
    tracked](/docs/ranks-and-stats/your-stats).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="note" title="Separate from Ubisoft">
  Parley Rank is Parley's own rating for Parley matches. It never changes your Ubisoft rank, and
  ranked play in the game never changes it. The two can drift apart, and that is normal.
</Aside>
