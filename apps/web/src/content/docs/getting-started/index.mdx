Parley is a Discord app that builds balanced Rainbow Six Siege teams from real
skill and telemetry, then automates scrims and tournaments. To use it, you
link your game account once and every command works from there.

<Aside type="note" title="Rainbow Six Siege only">
  Parley currently supports Rainbow Six Siege. Your Ubisoft, Xbox, or PlayStation profile is all you
  need.
</Aside>

## What you need

- A Discord server where you can add apps (or an admin who can).
- A Rainbow Six Siege account on PC, Xbox, or PlayStation.

## Set up in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Parley to your server">
    A server admin adds Parley from [parley.bot](https://parley.bot). This takes the Manage Server
    permission. There is nothing else to configure in Discord before players start using it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run any command">
    Type `/link`, `/profile`, or `/history` in any channel. The first time, Parley shows a **Welcome
    to Parley** card explaining why it needs your game account. Click **Accept & Continue** to agree
    to the [Terms of Service](https://parley.bot/terms) and [Privacy
    Policy](https://parley.bot/privacy).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your account">
    Parley continues straight into the linking wizard. Pick your platform, enter your in-game name,
    and confirm. The full walkthrough is on the [link your
    account](/docs/getting-started/link-your-account) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your profile">
    Run `/profile` to see your linked accounts and your Parley Rank. That is it: you are ready for
    matched play.
  </Step>
</Steps>
