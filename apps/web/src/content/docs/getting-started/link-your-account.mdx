Linking connects your Discord identity to your Rainbow Six Siege profile so
Parley can pull stats and rate your skill. The whole flow is menus only — no
command arguments.

## Walk through the wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run /link">
    Parley opens the **Link Your Accounts** hub. Choose **Add a platform** and
    pick PC, Xbox, or PlayStation.

    <LinkHubEmptyDemo />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your in-game name">
    A form asks for your username. Use the exact name for that platform (table
    below).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check platform and username, then **Continue** — or **Go back** to start
    over.

    <LinkConfirmDemo />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for your stats">
    Parley fetches your profile. After a moment you get **You're Ready To Play**
    with Parley Rank, level, and Rainbow Six rank.

    <LinkFetchingDemo />
    <LinkReadyDemo />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which name to enter

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="PC">
    Your Ubisoft Connect username. If you play through Steam, use the Ubisoft account name, not your
    Steam name.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Xbox">
    Your Xbox gamertag. This covers Xbox consoles and PC Game Pass accounts.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="PlayStation">Your PlayStation Network online ID.</TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside type="tip" title="One account per platform">
  You can link one Rainbow Six profile per platform: one PC, one Xbox, and one PlayStation. If a
  platform is already linked, the wizard returns you to the hub.
</Aside>

## See your links again

`/link` is also where you manage existing links — platform, username, Parley
Rank, and a menu to remove a link.

<LinkHubLinkedDemo />

<Aside type="caution" title="Re-link cooldown">
  After you remove a link, Parley makes you wait before linking again on that platform. The hub
  shows exactly when re-linking is available.
</Aside>

## If linking fails

<LinkFailedDemo />

A red **Link Failed** card means the stats provider could not resolve your
username. Check spelling, confirm the platform matches where you play, and try
again.

Full command reference: [/link](/docs/commands/link).
